## What it does

- Save worlds as you explore. Play as you normally would and the download follows where you go.
- Works on a multiplayer server, not in your own singleplayer or LAN world.
- Open the containers you want to keep. One you walked past but never opened is saved empty.
- Resume an interrupted download. Pick it up later and keep adding to the same world.
- Choose what gets saved. Turn entities, containers, and player data on or off before you start.
- Download from a ReplayMod or Flashback replay as well as from a live server.
- Runs on Fabric, NeoForge, and Quilt.
- Everything comes from your own client, so nothing runs on the server.
- Save a shareable backup. Zip a finished download into one file you can send to a friend. That zip carries your username and your inventory, so read [what a shared download reveals](/concepts/what-gets-downloaded/) before you send one.
